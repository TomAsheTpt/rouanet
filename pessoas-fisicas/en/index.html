01

How the deduction works

Brazil's federal culture incentive law, the Lei Rouanet, lets an individual taxpayer send part of the tax they would pay anyway to an approved cultural project. Favela Brass is one of them: PRONAC 265026, Article 18.

In practice, you give an amount to the project, you receive a recibo de mecenato (the official donation receipt), and you take 100% of that amount off your income tax in the following year's return, up to a limit of 6% of your tax due.

The benefit only exists for people who file the full return (modelo completo, by legal deductions), not the simplified one. If you file the simplified return, or you live outside Brazil, you can still support us with a direct gift, without the tax benefit. That option is further down this page.

R$ 10,000
gives R$ 600

If your income tax due for the year (imposto devido) is R$ 10,000, you can send up to R$ 600 to Favela Brass and deduct all of it. The real cost to you is zero, because it is tax you would pay in any case. To find your own number, use the calculator below.

02

Work out your limit

Your limit is 6% of your income tax due for the year. If you know that figure, type it in. If you don't, estimate it from your income.

Three ways to get the exact number

  1. From your last tax return. Open the summary (resumo), or the filing receipt, and find the line "Imposto devido". If your income and deductions are similar this year, 6% of that figure is your limit.
  2. From your accountant. Ask for an estimate of your 2026 imposto devido. It is the most exact figure there is before the return itself.
  3. In two steps. Give a safe amount now and top it up in the last week of December, once your year is known. Nothing can be added after 31 December.

06

Common questions

Do I have to file the full return?

Yes. The Lei Rouanet deduction only exists in the full return (modelo completo, by legal deductions). It does not apply to the simplified return, but you can still support us with a direct gift.

I'm a foreigner living in Brazil. Can I use this?

Yes. What counts is that you file a Brazilian income tax return in the full model and have tax due in Brazil. Your nationality makes no difference.

How much can I give?

Up to 6% of your income tax due for the year. That is 6% of the tax, not 6% of your income. There is no minimum. The calculator estimates your limit in seconds. The 6% limit is shared with any other incentive donations you make in the same year, such as the children's and adolescents', older people's and sports funds.

What if I go over the 6%?

Anything above the limit is still a donation to Favela Brass, but with no deduction: it does not come back in your refund and it does not carry over to the next year. The part within the limit is deducted as normal. If you are unsure, use the calculator or ask your accountant for the exact figure.

Does the new income tax exemption change anything?

Since 2026, people with taxable income of up to R$ 5,000 a month no longer pay income tax, and people earning up to R$ 7,350 pay less. If you no longer pay tax, there is nothing to send through the Lei Rouanet, but you can support us with a direct gift. If you pay less tax, your 6% limit is smaller. The calculator already applies the new rule.

Is the cost really zero?

Within the 6% limit, yes. The amount you give is taken in full off the tax you would pay in any case, so it goes to a child's music education where it would have gone to the government. The money leaves your account now and comes back in your 2027 return, as less tax to pay or a larger refund. You only need to give within the calendar year and keep the receipt for your next return.

How do I get the recibo de mecenato?

After sending the Pix by branch and account to the project's fundraising account, send the proof of transfer with your full name, CPF, full address with CEP, city, phone and e-mail, by WhatsApp to +55 21 99109-4882 or to tom@favelabrass.org. Favela Brass issues the recibo de mecenato, which is the document you use in your return.

What is the deadline?

To count for this year's income tax, the transfer has to be made by 31 December 2026, inside the calendar year. Please don't leave it to the last working days of the year: allow some margin so the transfer is processed and the receipt issued. After that date the donation counts for the following tax year.

I live outside Brazil or file the simplified return. Can I help?

You can. In those cases the route is the direct gift, with no tax benefit, open to any amount and to anyone. From abroad, use Favela Brass's PayPal; in Brazil, use the Pix for the direct gift.
